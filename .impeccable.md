## Design Context

### Users
Potential employers and clients: recruiters evaluating a full-stack / backend developer, and businesses with a process (Excel, WhatsApp, disconnected systems) they want turned into software. Viewed in a professional context; the page has to support both paths ("Busco talento" / "Tengo un proyecto").

### Brand Personality
Precise, curious, systems-minded. Confident and calm rather than flashy: the space metaphor (planets, orbits, layers) carries the idea "I see a system, not a screen".

### Aesthetic Direction
**Portfolio Espacial**: a deep night sky (`#030014`) with violet nebulae, a star canvas, shaded planets and orbit diagrams. Every token lives on `.space` in `src/styles/space.css`; `tailwind.config.mjs` only mirrors them.

- **Theme**: dark only
- **Colors**: bg `#030014`, ink `#EEEBFF`, soft `#C9C4EA`, dim `#A49FC9`, violet `#A78BFA` (primary accent), cyan `#7DE3FF` (focus, active, "after" states), amber `#FFC07A`, pink `#F9A8D4`, green `#6EF3A5` (current / available)
- **Fonts**: Syne (display, 600–800), Instrument Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels, data, diagrams)
- **Shapes**: panels at 16–20px radius, pill buttons, hairline violet borders (`--line`, `--line-soft`)
- **Motion**: `--ease: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`; pointer spotlight on panels; Lenis smooth scroll, all of it off under reduced motion

### Protected designs
Do not redesign these without asking: the MARIO YAEL heading (`Name.tsx`), the whole Hero (orrery, corner ribbon), Proyectos / Work and Proceso (path with waypoints).

### Design Principles
1. **Systems over screens**: diagrams (data flows, layers, before/after) explain the work better than adjectives
2. **Vary the rhythm**: not every section is heading + card grid; use editorial lists, split layouts and sticky headings
3. **Token discipline**: colors via the `.space` custom properties, never raw values in components
4. **Restraint in motion**: motion signals state or guides attention; nothing loops for decoration alone
5. **Bilingual by default**: every string exists in `es` and `en`
