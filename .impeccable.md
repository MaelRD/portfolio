## Design Context

### Users
Potential employers and clients looking for a backend developer. Viewed in a professional context — someone evaluating Mario's technical work and deciding whether to hire or collaborate.

### Brand Personality
Precise, dark, driven. Not flashy for its own sake — confident and editorial. Feels like a high-end tech publication, not a template.

### Aesthetic Direction
**The Noir Editorial** — dark background (#1D1616), cinematic red accents (#D84040), editorial typography. Think WIRED magazine cover meets luxury developer portfolio.

- **Theme**: Dark only
- **Colors**: obsidian `#1D1616`, cin-red `#D84040`, deep-red `#8E1616`, offwhite `#EEEEEE`, muted `#A3A3A3`, surface `#2A2A2A`
- **Fonts**: Manrope (headlines, bold/extrabold) + Onest (body, 300–600)
- **No rounded corners** on CTAs and interactive elements — sharp, editorial
- **Photo treatment**: rectangular portrait, not circular
- **Icons**: SVG, tinted to cin-red via SimpleIcons CDN

### Design Principles
1. **Editorial hierarchy** — Big typographic statements, not safe uniform grids
2. **Sharp over soft** — No border-radius on buttons/inputs; corners stay square
3. **Restraint in motion** — No decorative spinning/bouncing; motion only for state feedback
4. **Token discipline** — All colors via Tailwind tokens (obsidian, cin-red, etc.), never raw values
5. **Meaningful density** — Featured content gets space; secondary content stays compact
